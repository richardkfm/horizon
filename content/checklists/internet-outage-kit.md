# Internet and phone outage kit

Everything here is prepared *before* an outage, because each item is
something you would otherwise have to fetch over the connection you've just
lost. An hour with a pen and a charger covers most of it. Check it twice a
year, alongside the smoke alarms.

> **Tip:** Do the whole list on paper first. Paper needs no charge, no signal,
> and no login, and it is the layer people skip.

## On paper, in a sealed bag

- [ ] Phone numbers and addresses of family, neighbours, doctor, landlord
- [ ] One out-of-area contact everyone in the household can check in with
- [ ] Household plan: meeting point, who checks on whom, when to expect each other
- [ ] Local and national radio frequencies, taped to the radio
- [ ] Medicines, doses, allergies, and conditions for each person
- [ ] Account, policy, and insurance numbers
- [ ] Printed map of the area and your routes out
- [ ] A second copy kept somewhere else (relative, workplace)

## Radio and power

- [ ] Battery or wind-up radio, tested this year
- [ ] Sealed pack of fresh batteries stored with it
- [ ] Charged power bank and the right cables
- [ ] Solar or wind-up charger, if you have one
- [ ] Corded phone that needs no mains power, if your line still supports one

## Money

- [ ] Small cash float in **small notes and coins**
- [ ] Split between home, go-bag, and a pocket
- [ ] Written note of what you owe and what you're owed
- [ ] Notebook and pen for tabs and running totals

## Saved on your devices

- [ ] Offline maps downloaded for home, work, and anywhere you'd travel
- [ ] Key documents marked "available offline", not just bookmarked
- [ ] Photos of passport, ID, insurance, prescriptions
- [ ] Manuals for boiler, stove, pump, car
- [ ] One offline reference app that works with the network off

## Logins that won't lock you out

- [ ] Printed two-factor recovery codes, with the paper layer
- [ ] Password vault downloaded to the device, not sync-only
- [ ] Authenticator app (works offline) preferred over SMS codes

## For the street

- [ ] Somewhere agreed for a notice board, with a pen on a string
- [ ] Names and door numbers of the few neighbours you'd check on
- [ ] Who has a radio, a corded phone, a generator, or a car
- [ ] Anyone with a medical or care alarm that dials out over the network

## Twice a year

- [ ] Airplane mode drill: open maps, a document, the vault, a manual
- [ ] Replace stale batteries and refresh the cash float
- [ ] Update the paper copies — numbers change, people move
- [ ] Copy your saved documents onto a USB stick kept elsewhere
