# Seed-saving calendar

Seed saving is spread across the whole growing year: decisions in winter,
marking the best plants in summer, collecting and drying in autumn. Tick
through this season by season — the how-to is in [[food-seed-saving]].
Timings depend on your climate, so read "season" rather than a fixed month.

> **Tip:** Start with the easy, self-pollinating crops — beans, peas,
> lettuce, and tomatoes — before trying squash, brassicas, or corn, which
> cross easily.

## Winter (planning)

- [ ] Stored seed checked: still dry, labelled, and free of mould or pests
- [ ] Germination test done on older seed (a few seeds on a damp cloth)
- [ ] Open-pollinated or heirloom varieties chosen (not F1 hybrids)
- [ ] One variety per crop planned, or crops planned far enough apart to
      avoid crossing
- [ ] A seed swap arranged with neighbours or a local group

## Spring (sowing)

- [ ] Extra plants sown of crops you'll save seed from
- [ ] Seed rows or plants marked on your garden plan
- [ ] Labels with variety and sowing date

## Summer (choosing)

- [ ] The healthiest, most productive, best-tasting plants marked with a
      tie or stake
- [ ] Several plants kept for seed, not just one
- [ ] Off-type or diseased plants removed before they flower
- [ ] Lettuce and other leafy crops left to bolt and flower
- [ ] Bean and pea pods left on the plant to dry

## Late summer and autumn (collecting)

- [ ] Dry seeds (beans, peas, grains, lettuce) collected once pods and heads
      are dry and brittle
- [ ] Wet seeds (tomato, squash, cucumber) scooped from fully ripe fruit
- [ ] Tomato seed fermented a few days in water, then rinsed
- [ ] Seeds spread out to dry in a warm, airy, shaded place
- [ ] Seed snaps rather than bends before storing

## Storing

- [ ] Seed in airtight jars, tins, or sealed packets
- [ ] Every packet labelled with variety and year
- [ ] Stored cool, dark, and dry
- [ ] A little dry charcoal, rice, or ash in the jar to absorb moisture
- [ ] Notes kept on how each variety did, to guide next year's choices
- [ ] Spare seed shared or added to a community seed store
