# Infant and child emergency kit

Babies and small children need things adults don't, and shops may be shut or
far away when you most need them. Pack this alongside each person's
[[checklist:go-bag]], keep a bigger supply at home, and update it every few
months — children outgrow clothes and nappy sizes fast.

> **Tip:** Read [[health-diarrhoea-dehydration]] now — babies dehydrate
> quickly, and oral rehydration salts are the most useful thing in this kit.
> For a birth before help can arrive, see [[health-emergency-childbirth]].

## Feeding

- [ ] For breastfed babies: a comfortable carrier or sling, and extra water
      and snacks for the person breastfeeding
- [ ] For formula-fed babies: ready-to-feed liquid formula (needs no water)
      for at least three days
- [ ] Clean bottles and teats, or disposable cups for cup-feeding
- [ ] A way to boil water and clean bottles, and bottled or safe water
- [ ] Baby food or soft food, a spoon, and a bowl for older babies
- [ ] Easy snacks and a drinking cup for toddlers and children

## Nappies and hygiene

- [ ] Nappies for at least three days (in the current size)
- [ ] Wipes, nappy cream, and changing mat
- [ ] Bags for dirty nappies
- [ ] Hand sanitiser and soap
- [ ] Spare clothes and underwear for potty-training children

## Health

- [ ] Oral rehydration salts (sachets)
- [ ] A thermometer
- [ ] Children's pain and fever relief in the correct strength for their age,
      with an oral syringe
- [ ] Any regular medicines and the child's medicine list
      (see [[checklist:household-medicines]])
- [ ] Plasters and a small first-aid kit
- [ ] Sun cream, sun hat, and insect repellent suitable for children
- [ ] Vaccination record or a copy of the child's health book

## Warmth and sleep

- [ ] Warm layers, a hat, and socks; a waterproof layer
- [ ] A baby sleeping bag or blanket, and an emergency blanket
- [ ] A portable cot or travel bed for babies if you have room

## Comfort and documents

- [ ] A favourite toy, comforter, or blanket
- [ ] Small books, crayons, paper, a pack of cards
- [ ] A recent photo of each child, in case you're separated
- [ ] Each child's name, your phone number, and a contact person on a card
      in their pocket
- [ ] Copies of birth certificates or ID
