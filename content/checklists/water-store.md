# Water store

Water is the first thing you run short of and the hardest to do without. Aim to
store at least **4 litres (about 1 gallon) per person per day** — the common
emergency figure for drinking plus minimal cooking and hygiene — for a minimum
of a few days, more if you can. Store more in hot weather, and for anyone sick,
pregnant or breastfeeding, and for children. For a comfortable everyday budget
(washing, animals, garden) see [[calc-water-budget]]. Rotate stored water every
six months.

> **Tip:** Don't only *store* water — keep the means to *treat* more, so you're not
> limited to what's in the containers. See [[water-field-testing]] and
> [[water-choosing-treatment]].

## Stored water

- [ ] 4+ litres (about 1 gallon) per person per day, for at least 3 days
      (ideally 2 weeks) — more in heat, for the sick, pregnant or
      breastfeeding, and for children
- [ ] Clean, food-grade containers with tight lids
- [ ] Containers dated and rotated every 6 months
- [ ] A small, separate supply in your go-bag

## Treating more water

- [ ] Filter (gravity, pump, or straw type)
- [ ] Purification tablets or plain unscented household bleach (and a
      dropper) — replace bleach every 6–12 months
- [ ] A pot for boiling and the fuel to do it
- [ ] Clean cloth for pre-filtering cloudy water

## Collecting and carrying

- [ ] Buckets or jerry cans for fetching
- [ ] Clean sheeting or barrels for rainwater catchment
- [ ] Funnel and spare lids

## Hygiene with limited water

- [ ] Hand sanitiser and wet wipes
- [ ] A way to capture and reuse grey water for flushing
