# Home weatherproofing and retrofit

Work through this before winter, one room at a time — most items cost little
and take an afternoon. The how-to for each step is in
[[shelter-weatherproof-home]].

> **Tip:** Seal draughts, never ventilation. Air bricks, trickle vents,
> extractor fans, and the air supply to any boiler, stove, or fire must stay
> open. Fit a carbon monoxide alarm wherever fuel is burned.

## Draughts

- [ ] Draught hunt done on a windy day, with gaps marked
- [ ] Brush or rubber strip round each outside door and along the bottom
- [ ] Letterbox brush and keyhole cover fitted
- [ ] Foam or rubber strips on opening windows
- [ ] Gaps round window frames sealed with flexible sealant
- [ ] Gaps in floorboards and under skirting boards filled
- [ ] Loft hatch sealed round the edge, insulation on the lid
- [ ] Pipe and cable holes through outside walls filled
- [ ] Unused chimney fitted with a removable draught excluder (only if
      nothing burns there)

## Insulation

- [ ] Loft insulation topped up to the depth local guidance recommends
- [ ] Eaves kept clear so the loft stays ventilated
- [ ] Insulation kept clear of flues, chimneys, and recessed lights
- [ ] Loft pipes and water tank lagged
- [ ] Hot-water cylinder jacket fitted
- [ ] Exposed pipes in cold spaces lagged against freezing

## Windows and warmth

- [ ] Thick or lined curtains reaching the sill or floor
- [ ] Clear window film on single-glazed windows for winter
- [ ] Curtain or door over open stairwells and doorways
- [ ] Rugs on bare, cold floors
- [ ] A warm room chosen for evenings, with furniture away from cold walls

## Ventilation and damp

- [ ] Air bricks and trickle vents clear and open
- [ ] Extractor fans in kitchen and bathroom working
- [ ] Carbon monoxide alarm near every fuel-burning appliance
- [ ] Laundry dried outside or with a window open
- [ ] Any mould cleaned and its cause (leak or poor airflow) fixed

## Keeping the rain out

- [ ] Gutters and downpipes cleared
- [ ] Roof checked for slipped or missing tiles and damaged flashing
- [ ] Outside of window frames sealed
- [ ] Soil and paths kept below the damp-proof line of the walls
- [ ] Outside taps lagged or drained before frost
